# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript; open `index.html` directly, with no backend or build step.

## Users

People viewing Tyopoetra's personal portfolio to understand his current work and background.

## Product Purpose

A personal branding page that introduces Tyopoetra and makes his permitted contact channels easy to use.

## Positioning

Show current, documented cooker experience alongside earlier electrical training and unfinished online accounting study, without overstating culinary credentials.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is a responsive one-page personal website with navigation across an introduction, work experience, skills and education, certificates, and contact details. It is opened locally from `index.html`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Indonesian and English; dark and light themes; email, WhatsApp, and Instagram links. Certificate scans and the profile photo are optional local assets. No backend, form, hosting, or domain setup.

## Brand Commitments

Use Tyopoetra as the display name. Professional, minimalist, black-and-white design.

## Evidence on Hand

Candidate facts are supplied in the project brief. No local CV, profile photograph, or certificate image is present. Do not fabricate candidate details or imagery. Public contact permission covers email, WhatsApp, and Instagram.

## Product Principles

- Keep every public claim traceable to the supplied candidate data.
- Lead with current kitchen work and present other training as supporting background.
- Keep the page focused on verified professional experience and relevant background.
- Make contact and display preferences usable without a server.
